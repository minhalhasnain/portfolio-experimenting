Interactive Quiz — UBC Alumni Magazine

Where in the World Was This Photo Taken?

A collaborative interactive quiz built with my digital editor for UBC Alumni Magazine's My Town series, which highlights UBC alumni living around the world. Readers guess the location of a photo, then get a fact about that place along with a link to the full My Town story behind it.

My role: I wrote the intro body copy, plus the confirmation text for each question — the copy revealed once a reader selects their answer.

Sample confirmation text — Tokyo

A comforting dish that consists of pouring green tea or hot water over cooked rice, then adding an assortment of savoury toppings, ochazuke was one of Hannah Edward’s (BA’22) favourite meals during the three years she spent in Tokyo. Check out her My Town feature on Japan’s bustling capital if you’re thinking of visiting this popular tourist destination.

UBC eProjects

Launch Party

eProjects' first event of the year, built around a launch-to-space concept — the idea being that the club's ideas would be "launched" now and explored further as the year went on.

This became the club's first event to draw real buzz and attention on campus.

Process: hand-drawn sketch → digitally painted concept → final flat vector design, adapted for both a portrait poster and a wide LinkedIn/banner crop.
UBC eProjects — Flagship Event

Executive Lounge

eProjects' flagship event, bringing in 20 delegates to share their experience in their roles and as founders of their own companies. I collated the full branded guidebook, from cover to logistics, via Canva.

The most ambitious piece of event branding I've done for the club alongside my team — a multi-page guidebook rather than a single poster.

UBC eProjects

Accelerate

An event connecting students directly with startup accelerators — built on the idea that once ideas are formed, they need to be accelerated. I followed the initial poster with a "24 hours left" urgency post to drive last-minute turnout.

A two-part push: an announcement poster, then a countdown reminder closer to the date.

Process: the poster's color story started as an ideation/gradient study before landing on the final blue-to-red palette. Designed in Adobe Fresco and Canva.
UBC eProjects — Brand Refresh

eBulb Mascot Revamp

eProjects' mascot, eBulb, needed to be modernized to match the club's updated "e" lightbulb logo. I collaborated with a teammate on mascot ideation, working through color, eyes, blush, and base options before landing on the final design.

A deliberate design-system approach rather than a single redraw: every variable was tested before the final mascot was locked in.

Process: old mascot → build-your-own ideation sheet (color, eyes, blush, base options tested systematically) → final revamped mascot matching the modern logo. Designed in Adobe Fresco and Illustrator.
UBC eProjects — Brand Identity

Sticker Series

A set of five digital stickers designed to capture the club's energy and constant innovation — artistic and expressive rather than a plain brand logo.

Process: hand-drawn digitally in Adobe Fresco.
