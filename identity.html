Identity

A body of work exploring hidden, anonymous, and cultural identity — through masks, artist studies, and a final self-portrait. Part of the Visual Art portfolio →

Vibrant, multicultural identities and hidden faces shrouded in mysteries are not an orthodox pair. My research and development, however, seeked to find the connection between them. By exploring faded faces, masked identities, and the impacts of culture on a person, I worked toward developing a final piece by bringing together three key aspects of identity: masked, faded, and cultural.

Final piece — Oil on canvas
Development: artist studies (Zin Lim, Eric Carrazedo, Alserkal Avenue exhibition, Guerrilla Girls), mask research, colour and process experiments, and a final photoshoot.
