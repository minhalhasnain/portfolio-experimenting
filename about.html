Karachi – Dubai – Vancouver. Three cities that shaped how I see, and how I make.

Journey

I thrive in the space where storytelling, creative design, and visual art intersect.

Legend has it my first word was paint.
My second one was right.

But now that I think about it, I probably meant write.

Turns out that's been the throughline ever since: visual storytelling, where images and words do the work together. I trace my interest in it back to three cities. I grew up surrounded by family and Karachi's culture. Then, I moved to Dubai's multicultural city life. Finally, I landed in biophilic Vancouver. Every move inspired a new experience, and every experience inspires a new creation.

When I'm not living in my fast-paced, creative world, I'm trying to hit 20 push-ups, playing Yahtzee with my family, and catching up on classic movies – thankfully not all at the same time.
